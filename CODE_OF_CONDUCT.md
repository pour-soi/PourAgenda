# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Participants must be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, publication of another person’s private information, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

Examples of positive participation include:

- focusing feedback on code and observable behavior;
- welcoming different experience levels and perspectives;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility;
- protecting credentials, personal data, and private vulnerability reports.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

For confidential platform abuse or safety concerns, use GitHub’s **Report abuse or spam** controls. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public conduct report.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, history, and the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
